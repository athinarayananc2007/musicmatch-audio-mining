How MusicMatch Listens to Sound

Analyzing the fundamental timbre, cadence, harmony, and frequency characteristics of songs to help editors and listeners curate with precision.

Deep Acoustic Profiling

Evaluates timbral texture, vocal resonance, rhythmic tempo (BPM), and acoustic brightness directly from the sound waveform.

Harmonic Playlist Grouping

Visual Sound Map & Similarity

How MusicMatch Finds Your Match

Four intuitive stages transform your audio tracks into curated playlists and recommendations.

01

Upload Audio

Upload your music tracks in MP3 or WAV format, or record directly using your microphone.

02

Acoustic Listening

MusicMatch analyzes the opening soundscape, measuring beat speed (BPM), frequency brightness, warmth, and texture.

03

Smart Grouping

Tracks are balanced across all sound traits and organized into cohesive mood-based playlists.

04

Discover & Explore

Click any song to discover acoustically matching tracks and explore relative distances on the visual sound map.
