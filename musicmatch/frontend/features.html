Platform Features

Built for Music Editors & True Music Lovers

Discover, curate, and organize music based on the pure sound of every track—not tags, algorithms, or popularity metrics.

Smart Acoustic Matches

Discovers songs that genuinely match in rhythm, energy, and mood. Whether you need seamless song transitions for a mix or want to discover new favorite tracks, recommendations are based entirely on audio compatibility.

Mood-Based Playlists

Organize mixed audio libraries into cohesive, mood-consistent sets automatically. Ambient soundscapes, high-energy dance tracks, acoustic ballads, and chill lo-fi beats find their natural groupings.

Deep Sonic Profiling

Listens to the subtle vocal warmth, instrument textures, beat speed, and acoustic resonance that define each song’s sonic signature, giving editors and curators deeper musical insight.

Harmonic Playlist Sorting

Automatically sorts diverse song collections into harmonically aligned playlists. Tailor the number of groups to split a massive library into distinct vibe sets with a single click.

Cross-Genre Discovery

Uncover sonic twins and unexpected genre-crossing matches that share the exact same rhythm, tempo, and feel—perfect for beat-matching, video scoring, or discovering new music.

Drag & Drop Simplicity

Drag and drop your MP3 or WAV files, or record live from your microphone. Fast, private, and secure in-browser processing with zero complex setup.
